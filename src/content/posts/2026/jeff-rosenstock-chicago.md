Thanks to a heads up from [kthull](https://www.drupal.org/u/kthull) I was able to grab a ticket to the second night of Jeff Rosenstock's week long Chicago residency when a set of extra tickets opened up.

This was my third time seeing Jeff. The first was also at the Metro opening for AJJ just after his first solo album came out. Second was in Boston opening for Frank Turner after Post- was released. That was a one off show, so he didn't have his usual band and played mostly accoustic. Not really my first choice, but still a cool show.

All that to say that it was great to see him headlining and absolutely crushing it years later with even more great albums to pull from. [Setlist was crazy](https://www.setlist.fm/setlist/jeff-rosenstock/2026/metro-chicago-il-4348eb23.html) - 38 songs!
