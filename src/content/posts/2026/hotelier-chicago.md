A few weeks ago I caught the first show of [The Hotelier's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hotelier) "Goodness" 10th Anniversary tour at [Lincoln Hall](https://lh-st.com/) in Chicago. [Goodnees](https://open.spotify.com/album/1VFmbbxsbFtCeP3hCvOQAq) is probably in my top 10 albums of all time and The Hotelier doesn't tour as often these days, so it was well worth a trip into the city on a weeknight. It was the first time they had ever played the album in full.
