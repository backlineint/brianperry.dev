For my son's most recent birthday we got him a new bike. He's 15 so it is still his primary mode of transportation with friends, and he also bikes to school when it is warm enough. He didn't ride it a ton until it got warmer, but when he did he complained that it was slower than his friends bikes and wasn't able to maintain speed. It was a source of family controversy for a while - on one extreme I assumed he wasn't gear shifting correctly, and on the other there was talk about getting a new bike. Eventually cooler heads prevailed and we decided we should take it somewhere for a tune up before we decided to do anything.

Some of the obvious options were the local bike shop and the Dick's sporting goods that we got the bike from, but my searches also turned up something called [Beers for Gears](https://beersforgears.com/) that got my attention for self-explanatory reasons. The headline on the site is "Get your bike tuned up and put beers in my fridge". Ok, I'm listening.

I'll go right to the source to explain this particular side-hustle.

> Beers For Gears started as a pet project for me. My day job has me working a lot (but never more than I want to!) and I needed an outlet.

> One thing I have also gotten pretty good at doing is finding new beers that I like. And that has been fun!

> So I decided to put those two things together. I offer a resource for people who need simple maintenance done to their bike(s). I also want to stock my beer fridge.

> Therein lies the beauty of Beers for Gears. You bring me your bike. I fix it. You compensate me in cash, beer or some combination of the two. I have rates, and I can negotiate as well. It’s a pretty sweet gig.
